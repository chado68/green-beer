Our triage process

  1. Intake minutes

    You call the hotline or submit the triage form. We capture who is affected, what was seen, when it was detected and how much of the business is impacted, and assign a provisional priority.

  2. Responder call P1: within 1 hour

    A senior responder calls back to confirm priority, set up a secure out-of-band channel (attackers may be reading your email) and walk your team through immediate do's and don'ts.

  3. Contain and preserve

    We guide isolation of affected systems and accounts while preserving logs, memory and disk evidence for forensics, insurance and possible law enforcement referral.

  4. Scope and assess

    We determine the entry point, affected systems and data, and whether the attacker still has access. This drives every decision that follows.

  5. Notify and coordinate

    We help you engage your cyber insurance carrier, breach counsel and regulators on time. For Nevada gaming licensees this includes Gaming Control Board cyber incident reporting under Regulation 5.260.

  6. Recover and report

    We support safe restoration, verify the threat is gone, and deliver an executive summary and a technical report with lessons learned and hardening priorities.
