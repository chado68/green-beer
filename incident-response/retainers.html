Common questions

Does this work with my cyber insurance?

Yes. Many carriers require you to use a panel firm for the forensic investigation. Our oversight service is built for exactly that: we work alongside the panel firm on your behalf. Ask your carrier about pre-approval before an incident.

What if we never have an incident?

Unused prepaid hours on Professional and Enterprise convert to readiness work: plan updates, tabletops, log reviews or a security assessment.

Can you help with Nevada gaming requirements?

Yes. We help licensees prepare for and meet the cyber incident reporting and assessment expectations of Nevada Gaming Commission Regulation 5.260.
