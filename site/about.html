About

Defense-grade experience, now working for Nevada businesses.

CSI Cyber Solutions is a veteran-owned cybersecurity consultancy based in the Las Vegas valley.

Our story

CSI Cyber Solutions was founded on a simple idea: the security discipline that protects military and allied networks should be available to the businesses that keep our communities running.

Our founder spent 37 years in information technology and cybersecurity across the U.S. Department of Defense and NATO, including service with the Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA) and the Defense Logistics Agency (DLA). That career began with enlistment in the U.S. Navy in 1988 and grew into senior cybersecurity advisory work protecting networks that support operations across the U.S. and Europe.

Today that experience is focused on organizations in Nevada and the Pacific Northwest: defense contractors, casinos and resorts, hospitality groups, and growing businesses that need senior security leadership without the enterprise price tag.
